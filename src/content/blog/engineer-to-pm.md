## Outline

1. **Where I started.** A mechanical engineering degree, then R&D work with Japanese clients.
2. **The switch to software.** Building full-stack features that were really experiments.
3. **The switch to product.** Why I moved from building the thing to deciding which thing to build.
4. **What carried over.** Hypotheses, edge cases, and talking to engineers in their own language.
5. **What I had to unlearn.** Wanting to solve every problem myself.
